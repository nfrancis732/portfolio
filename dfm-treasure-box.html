Design for Manufacturing Project: CNC Milled Aluminum "Treasure Box"

The inspiration for this project is to protect a Guatemalan worry doll as she sleeps under your pillow and takes away your worries.

Design Requirements

Our most important design requirement for this project was achieving a running with for the diamond and dowel pins in the box, so that the top could easily be placed and removed from the bottom. Because of a mix-up with the reamers when machining the bottom of the box, we ended up having to use two dowel pins instead of a diamond and a dowel. This meant that tight tolerances and proper running fit clearances were especially important to ensuring our box was functional.

Results

After switching to a dowel pin instead of a diamond pin in the bottom piece if the box, we had to use a drill press to increase the diameter of a hole on the top piece to create enough clearance for a running fit between the dowels and the pins. Aside from the switch from a diamond pin to a dowel pin, we acheived the fit that we were aiming for as well as a seamless transition between the top and bottom of the box and consistent design aesthetics.

Skills Gained

CAM in Fusion, CNC and Conversational Mill, Tolerance, clearance, and fit calculations, Introduction to GD&T.
